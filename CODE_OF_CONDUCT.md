# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Chitti is committed to creating an open, welcoming, and respectful community for everyone who contributes, regardless of experience level, background, or role.

We believe great software is built through collaboration, respect, and constructive discussion.

---

## Expected Behaviour

Contributors are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally
- Welcome different perspectives
- Provide constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the project
- Help others learn and improve
- Respect community members and maintainers

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment or discrimination
- Personal attacks
- Trolling or disruptive behaviour
- Publishing private information without permission
- Unprofessional communication
- Deliberately harming the project or community

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it privately to the project maintainers.

Reports will be reviewed carefully and handled with appropriate confidentiality.

---

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Maintaining a respectful environment
- Reviewing reports fairly
- Taking appropriate action when needed
- Protecting the interests of the community

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- GitHub issues
- Pull requests
- Discussions
- Documentation
- Community channels
- Any official Chitti-related activity

---

## Thank You

Every contributor helps make Chitti better.

Thank you for helping build a secure, transparent, and modern community finance platform.
